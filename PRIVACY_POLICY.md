# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 24, 2026

Agri-Mizane ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Agri-Mizane (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- Full name
- Email address
- Profile photo (optional, if signing in with Google)

### 1.2 Farm & Agricultural Data
The App allows you to store and manage the following data related to your farming operations:
- Farm names, descriptions, and locations
- Parcel (field) information, including names, areas, crops, and statuses
- Financial records: expenses, revenues/incomes, gas usage, and cooperative support entries
- Member roles and invitations within your farms

### 1.3 Device & Usage Information
We may collect:
- Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers
- Push notification tokens (managed through OneSignal)
- App usage analytics and crash reports

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve the App's functionality
- Authenticate your identity and manage your account
- Sync your data across devices
- Send push notifications related to farm activity (e.g., new expenses, member joins)
- Communicate with you regarding account-related matters

## 3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using **Firebase (Google Cloud)** infrastructure. We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Firebase Authentication for secure sign-in
- Firestore security rules to restrict data access to authorized users only

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared only in the following circumstances:
- **Within your farm:** Data you add to a farm is accessible to other members of that farm based on their assigned roles.
- **Service providers:** We use third-party services (Firebase, OneSignal) that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality obligations.
- **Legal requirements:** We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid legal processes.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** your personal data stored in the App
- **Correct** any inaccurate data
- **Delete** your account and associated data at any time
- **Export** your data by contacting us
- **Opt out** of push notifications through your device settings

## 7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data from our active systems within a reasonable timeframe. Some data may persist in backup systems for a limited period due to technical constraints.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the App and updating the "Effective Date" above. Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
